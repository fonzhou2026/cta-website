The problem问题

Decades of through-traffic, under some of Kowloon East's busiest new districts.未来几十年的过境车流，压在九龙东最繁忙的新区之下。

The Kowloon Bay Section runs mostly underground through the high-density areas of Kai Tak and Cha Kwo Ling. Once open, it will carry heavy east–west traffic for decades — and the exhaust from its tunnel leaves through two ventilation buildings in the middle of the city.九龙湾段以隧道为主，穿越启德、茶果岭一带的高密度城区。通车后，它将长期承载大量东西向过境车流——而隧道的尾气，要经由城市中的两座通风大楼排出。

Released untreated, those particles and gases would land on the residents, students and office workers around them.若不经处理直接排放，这些颗粒物和有害气体将落在周边居民、学生和上班族身上。

Where it sits项目位置

The link that completes Route 6.让六号干线贯通的那一段。

West, it joins the Yau Ma Tei Section — a separate project, also purified by CTA. East, it meets the Tseung Kwan O–Lam Tin Tunnel. Interchanges at Kai Tak and Lam Tin.西接中九龙绕道（油麻地段）——另一个同样由 CTA 负责空气净化的项目；东连将军澳—蓝田隧道，在启德和蓝田设有交通交汇处。

Two sections, two CTA projects两个路段，两个 CTA 项目

One Central Kowloon Route — contracted and installed as two projects.贯通后统称中九龙绕道——签约和安装是两个独立项目。

  • CKRCentral Kowloon Route (Yau Ma Tei Section)中九龙绕道（油麻地段）
  • T2Central Kowloon Route (Kowloon Bay Section) — formerly Trunk Road T2 and the Cha Kwo Ling Tunnel中九龙绕道（九龙湾段）——前称 T2 主干路及茶果岭隧道

Route 6 is a key part of Hong Kong's strategic road network: a main artery between West Kowloon and Tseung Kwan O New Town, with interchanges at Kai Tak and Lam Tin. It also supports major developments — the West Kowloon Cultural District, the Hong Kong section of the Guangzhou–Shenzhen–Hong Kong Express Rail Link at West Kowloon Station, the cruise terminal, the Kai Tak Development, the Kowloon East core business district, and housing and industry in Tseung Kwan O.六号干线是香港策略性道路网络的重要部分，为西九龙至将军澳新市镇之间提供一条主要干道，并在启德和蓝田设有交通交汇处。它也是支援西九文化区、广深港高铁香港段西九龙站、邮轮码头、启德发展区、九龙东核心商业区以及将军澳住宅和工业发展的重要公路基建。

The Yau Ma Tei Section is 4.7 km long with a dual three-lane carriageway, including a tunnel of about 3.9 km, linking the Yau Ma Tei Interchange in West Kowloon with the Kai Tak Development and the Kowloon Bay road network in East Kowloon. With the Kowloon Bay Section complete, Route 6 opens in full: the peak-hour journey from Tseung Kwan O town centre to Yau Ma Tei is expected to fall from about 65 minutes before Route 6 to about 12 minutes.中九龙绕道（油麻地段）全长 4.7 公里，采用双程三线分隔车道，包括长约 3.9 公里的隧道，连接西九龙的油麻地交汇处与东九龙的启德发展区及九龙湾道路网，是横跨中九龙的干道。中九龙绕道（九龙湾段）完成后，六号干线将全线通车。届时，繁忙时段往返将军澳市中心及油麻地的行车时间，预计将由六号干线兴建前约 65 分钟大幅减至约 12 分钟。

What CTA builtCTA 做了什么

Active purification — not just dilution.主动净化，而不只是稀释。

Instead of relying only on ventilation and natural dispersion, the West Ventilation Building treats the tunnel exhaust centrally before it is released. CTA designed, manufactured, installed and commissioned the system.不再单纯依靠通风稀释和自然扩散，西通风大楼在废气排放前对其进行集中处理。系统由 CTA 负责设计、生产、安装及调试。

  1. Filter过滤

    High-efficiency electrostatic filtration removes fine particles.高效静电过滤去除细颗粒物。

  2. Remove gases去除有害气体

    A gas-removal stage treats harmful gases in the exhaust.气体去除环节处理尾气中的有害气体。

  3. Adapt动态调节

    Real-time monitoring adjusts operation to traffic volume and pollutant levels; automatic washing keeps efficiency stable under heavy load.实时监测根据车流量和污染物浓度动态调整运行；自动清洗让系统在高负荷下保持稳定高效。

The team团队

Five and a half years, three cities, one tight ventilation building.五年半，三座城市，一座空间紧张的通风大楼。

From system design through manufacture, installation and commissioning, CTA and AQT teams in Shanghai, Hong Kong and Norway worked with all parties to fit and integrate large equipment within the limited space of the ventilation building — on time and to specification. CTA continues to provide operation, maintenance and quality assurance.从系统设计到生产、安装和调试，来自上海、香港和挪威的 CTA 与爱优特团队跨地协作，与各方密切配合，在通风大楼有限的空间内完成大型设备的安装与系统联调，按时按质交付。此后 CTA 还将为系统提供运营维护与质量保证。

Applications应用方式

Designed around the tunnel.围绕隧道本身来设计。

Each station is shaped by the tunnel's construction, the purpose of the APS and the space available.每座净化站都由隧道结构、净化目标与可用空间决定。

Applications应用方式

One technology. Four ways to fit any tunnel.一套技术，四种安装方式，适配各类隧道。

{{include diagram-bypass.svg}}
A

Bypass installation旁路式安装

Polluted air is forced through a crescent-shaped side tunnel containing the precipitation equipment. Used at Granfoss, Ekeberg and Lærdal in Norway and Chinbu in South Korea.污染空气被引入装有除尘设备的新月形侧向旁路隧道中处理。应用于挪威 Granfoss、Ekeberg、洛达尔隧道及韩国 Chinbu 隧道。

  • Higher system efficiency系统效率更高
  • Lower installation & operating cost安装与运行成本更低
  • ~30% less rock excavation岩石开挖量减少约 30%
{{include diagram-ceiling.svg}}
B

Ceiling-mounted installation顶置式安装

Fans and filtration equipment are mounted overhead at intervals. Installed at Hell Tunnel (Trondheim), Nygård Tunnel (Bergen) and Strømsås Tunnel (Drammen).风机与过滤设备按间距安装于隧道顶部。应用于特隆赫姆 Hell 隧道、卑尔根 Nygård 隧道、德拉门 Strømsås 隧道。

  • Lower construction cost土建成本更低
  • Large smoke evacuation in fire火灾排烟能力强
  • Tested with smoke at 200 °C通过 200 °C 烟气测试
{{include diagram-shaft.svg}}
C

Shaft installation竖井式安装

At Festning Tunnel, Oslo — Norway's busiest — three fans move over 200,000 m³/h of cleaned air through a ventilation shaft, protecting the local population from traffic pollution.在挪威最繁忙的奥斯陆 Festning 隧道，3 台风机每小时将超过 200,000 m³ 净化后的空气经通风竖井排出，保护周边居民免受交通污染。

  • Outlet only ~1 m high排风口仅约 1 米高
  • 7–9 m/s vs. 25 m/s exit speed出口风速 7–9 m/s（原需 25 m/s）
{{include diagram-distributed.svg}}
D

Distributed ceiling-mounted分布式顶置安装

Compact air cleaners distributed along tunnels with limited space — especially existing tunnels that need pollution control without major civil works.紧凑型净化单元沿隧道分布安装，适用于空间受限的隧道，尤其是无需大规模土建改造的既有运营隧道。

  • Flexible location & quantity位置与数量灵活
  • Fits existing, operating tunnels可用于既有运营隧道
  • High efficiency at high air speed高风速下仍保持高效

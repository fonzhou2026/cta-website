The problem问题

A long urban tunnel concentrates traffic exhaust — and has to breathe out somewhere.长距离城市隧道会把尾气聚集起来——而它总得从某个地方排出去。

Inside a tunnel, emissions from thousands of vehicles build up: fine particles, carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons and nitrogen oxides. Ventilation pushes this air out through ventilation buildings. In Kowloon, those buildings stand among homes, schools and offices.隧道内，成千上万辆车的排放不断累积：细颗粒物、一氧化碳、碳氢化合物、氮氧化物。通风系统把这些空气经通风大楼排出——而在九龙，这些通风大楼就矗立在住宅、学校和写字楼之间。

Dilution alone moves the problem from the tunnel to the neighbourhood. The Central Kowloon Route needed the exhaust treated before release.仅靠稀释，只是把问题从隧道里搬到了街坊身边。中九龙绕道需要的是：在排放之前先把尾气处理掉。

Where it sits项目位置

The western third of Route 6.六号干线的西段。

Route 6 links West Kowloon with Tseung Kwan O. Its two sections under Kowloon form one Central Kowloon Route — two separate projects, both purified by CTA.六号干线连接西九龙与将军澳。它在九龙地下的两段贯通后统称中九龙绕道——这是两个独立项目，空气净化都由 CTA 交付。

What CTA builtCTA 做了什么

Nine stations, three buildings, one system — CTA's largest project to date.9 座净化站、3 座通风大楼、1 套系统——CTA 迄今规模最大的项目。

  1. Capture收集

    Exhaust air from the tunnel is drawn into the air purification stations inside the three ventilation buildings.隧道内的污染空气被引入三座通风大楼内的空气净化站。

  2. Clean净化

    High-efficiency electrostatic filtration removes fine particles before the air is released — 2,250 m³/s across nine stations.高效静电过滤在排放前去除细颗粒物——9 座站合计处理风量 2,250 m³/s。

  3. Keep it clean保持高效

    Automatic washing keeps the filters at full efficiency; a PLC control system runs the stations.自动清洗让过滤器保持高效；PLC 控制系统统一运行各净化站。

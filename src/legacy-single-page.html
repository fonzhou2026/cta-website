Company关于 CTA

Three decades of clean air, engineered for tunnels.三十余年，
只专注隧道空气净化。

Clean Tunnel Air International AS was founded in 1989 in Trondheim, Norway. For more than 30 years CTA has been a world leader in the development and provision of air purification for road tunnels, with more than 20 air purification stations completed in more than 10 tunnels worldwide. Clean Tunnel Air International AS（CTA）1989 年创立于挪威特隆赫姆。三十余年来，CTA 一直是公路隧道空气净化领域的全球引领者，已在全球 10 余条隧道中建成 20 余座空气净化站。

CTA specialises in designing, developing, manufacturing and installing turnkey electrostatic dust precipitation and gas removal systems — with unique technology that minimises the space a station needs inside the tunnel while keeping efficiency high. CTA 专注于交钥匙式静电除尘与气体去除系统的设计、研发、制造和安装，独有技术可在保持高效率的同时，最大限度减少净化站在隧道内的占用空间。

Why air purification stations为什么需要隧道空气净化

Cleaner air inside. Cleaner city outside.隧道内更洁净，城市里更清新。

Engine particles are mostly smaller than 1 μm, with a median diameter of about 0.2 μm — by far the most dangerous of the commonly found particles. In an unfiltered urban tunnel we breathe them at high concentration every time we drive through.发动机排放的颗粒物大多小于 1 μm，中位粒径约 0.2 μm，是常见颗粒物中危害最大的一类。在未经净化的城市隧道中，每次通行我们都在高浓度地吸入它们。

Protect people's health保护通行人员健康

Removing ultrafine engine particles where exposure is highest — inside the tunnel.在暴露浓度最高的隧道内部，直接去除超细发动机颗粒物。

Protect the surrounding city保护周边环境

Cleaning air before it leaves the portal. At M30 Madrid, treated air is cleaner than the city's normal air.在排出洞口前完成净化。马德里 M30 项目中，处理后的空气甚至优于城市日常空气质量。

Improve visibility提升隧道能见度

90–98% of particles above 0.1 μm removed, keeping visibility within requirements and reducing accidents.去除 90–98% 的 0.1 μm 以上颗粒，保持能见度达标，降低交通事故风险。

Clear smoke in a fire火灾时排烟

Systems keep working for 20–30 minutes in a fire, removing smoke so people can find the exits and rescuers can enter.火灾时系统可持续运行 20–30 分钟清除烟雾，帮助人员寻找出口、便于救援。

Avoid tall exhaust stacks无需高耸排风塔

With purification, the exhaust outlet can be just one metre high — blending into the landscape and saving construction cost.配置净化系统后，排风口高度仅需约 1 米，与环境融为一体，并节省土建成本。

Save fan energy大幅节约风机能耗

Instead of diluting pollution at 25 m/s exit speed, air can leave at about 7 m/s — a major saving on axial-fan energy.无需以 25 m/s 高速稀释排放，出口风速可降至约 7 m/s，大幅降低轴流风机能耗。

Technology核心技术

Mechanical & inertial pre-filters机械与惯性预过滤器

Pre-filters are either panel filters for removing dust, or inertial maze filters with their own fans. As the dust-laden air enters the maze, most of the air changes direction and separates from the dust as it passes through the vanes.预过滤器可选用面板式机械过滤器除尘，或自带风机的惯性迷宫过滤器。含尘空气进入迷宫后，气流在叶片间改变方向，与粉尘分离。

High-efficiency spike ionizer ESP高效尖端电离静电除尘器

Fine dust passes through a strong electrostatic field generated by CTA's spike ionizer and becomes electrically charged. In the following collector with interleaved plates, charged particles accumulate while clean air passes through with very little pressure drop.细颗粒物经过 CTA 尖端电离器形成的强电场后带电，随后在交错极板收集段中被吸附；洁净空气以极低压降通过。

Cells are built from stainless steel and aluminium in a modular design. The filter array can fill the entire cross-section of the bypass tunnel for maximum efficiency at high velocity.电场单元采用不锈钢与铝材模块化制造，可铺满旁路隧道整个断面，实现高风速下的最高效率。

Large axial fans大直径轴流风机

Axial fans draw polluted air from the tunnel into the filter system and return cleaned air to the tunnel. CTA uses very large fans — lowering running cost and giving a more uniform air profile over the filter banks for stable efficiency.轴流风机将污染空气从隧道抽入过滤系统，再将洁净空气送回隧道。CTA 采用超大直径风机，降低运行成本，同时使过滤段气流分布更均匀、效率更稳定。

Automatic washing system全自动清洗系统

Water and detergent tanks, a pump system, a dosing pump and a distribution network wash the electrostatic filters automatically. CTA supplies its own environmentally friendly detergent. Applications应用方式

One technology. Four ways to fit any tunnel.一套技术，四种安装方式，适配各类隧道。

Each station is designed around the tunnel's construction, the purpose of the APS and the space available.每座净化站都根据隧道结构形式、净化目标与空间条件量身设计。

A

Bypass installation旁路式安装

Polluted air is forced through a crescent-shaped side tunnel containing the precipitation equipment. Used at Granfoss, Ekeberg and Lærdal in Norway and Chinbu in South Korea.污染空气被引入装有除尘设备的新月形侧向旁路隧道中处理。应用于挪威 Granfoss、Ekeberg、洛达尔隧道及韩国 Chinbu 隧道。

  • Higher system efficiency系统效率更高
  • Lower installation & operating cost安装与运行成本更低
  • ~30% less rock excavation岩石开挖量减少约 30%
B

Ceiling-mounted installation顶置式安装

Fans and filtration equipment are mounted overhead at intervals. Installed at Hell Tunnel (Trondheim), Nygård Tunnel (Bergen) and Strømsås Tunnel (Drammen).风机与过滤设备按间距安装于隧道顶部。应用于特隆赫姆 Hell 隧道、卑尔根 Nygård 隧道、德拉门 Strømsås 隧道。

  • Lower construction cost土建成本更低
  • Large smoke evacuation in fire火灾排烟能力强
  • Tested with smoke at 200 °C通过 200 °C 烟气测试
C

Shaft installation竖井式安装

At Festning Tunnel, Oslo — Norway's busiest — three fans move over 200,000 m³/h of cleaned air through a ventilation shaft, protecting the local population from traffic pollution.在挪威最繁忙的奥斯陆 Festning 隧道，3 台风机每小时将超过 200,000 m³ 净化后的空气经通风竖井排出，保护周边居民免受交通污染。

  • Outlet only ~1 m high排风口仅约 1 米高
  • 7–9 m/s vs. 25 m/s exit speed出口风速 7–9 m/s（原需 25 m/s）
D

Distributed ceiling-mounted分布式顶置安装

Compact air cleaners distributed along tunnels with limited space — especially existing tunnels that need pollution control without major civil works.紧凑型净化单元沿隧道分布安装，适用于空间受限的隧道，尤其是无需大规模土建改造的既有运营隧道。

  • Flexible location & quantity位置与数量灵活
  • Fits existing, operating tunnels可用于既有运营隧道
  • High efficiency at high air speed高风速下仍保持高效

In focus · Hong Kong Route 6专题 · 香港六号干线

One road across Kowloon. Two CTA systems under it.一条横贯九龙的干道，地下两套 CTA 系统。

Route 6 links West Kowloon with Tseung Kwan O. Its two sections under Kowloon now form one Central Kowloon Route, but were contracted and installed as two separate projects — and CTA delivered the air purification for both.六号干线连接西九龙与将军澳。它在九龙地下的两段贯通后统称中九龙绕道，但签约和安装是分开进行的两个项目——空气净化系统都由 CTA 交付。

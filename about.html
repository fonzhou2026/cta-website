只专注隧道空气净化。

Clean Tunnel Air International AS was founded in 1989 in Trondheim, Norway. For more than 30 years CTA has been a world leader in the development and provision of air purification for road tunnels, with more than 20 air purification stations completed in more than 10 tunnels worldwide. Clean Tunnel Air International AS（CTA）1989 年创立于挪威特隆赫姆。三十余年来，CTA 一直是公路隧道空气净化领域的全球引领者，已在全球 10 余条隧道中建成 20 余座空气净化站。

CTA specialises in designing, developing, manufacturing and installing turnkey electrostatic dust precipitation and gas removal systems — with unique technology that minimises the space a station needs inside the tunnel while keeping efficiency high. CTA 专注于交钥匙式静电除尘与气体去除系统的设计、研发、制造和安装，独有技术可在保持高效率的同时，最大限度减少净化站在隧道内的占用空间。

Our mission我们的使命

To develop, manufacture and market total systems for the control of air pollution in road tunnels.开发、制造并推广公路隧道空气污染控制的整体系统解决方案。

Technology & expertise核心技术与专长

  • In-depth knowledge of electrostatic filtration technology and applications深厚的静电过滤技术及工程应用积累
  • Unique technology that minimises bypass tunnel area through high-efficiency filtration at high air speed高风速下高效过滤，显著缩小旁路隧道断面
  • Sophisticated waste-water recycling with an integrated PLC that controls the process automatically集成 PLC 自动控制的先进废水循环系统
  • R&D with universities and system partners — gas cleaning in Spain, filters for allergy and asthma patients in Austria与高校及系统伙伴联合研发——西班牙气体净化、奥地利过敏/哮喘人群专用过滤器

Part of AirQuality Group爱优特集团成员

Norwegian tunnel know-how, backed by a global air-technology group.挪威的隧道经验，加上全球化的空气技术集团。

In 2020 CTA joined AirQuality Technology (AQT), Shanghai. Combining CTA's tunnel engineering with AQT's manufacturing and market reach, the two deliver tailored solutions for transport infrastructure and large urban environmental projects worldwide.2020 年，CTA 并入爱优特空气技术（上海）有限公司（AQT）。双方在技术与市场上深度融合，为全球交通基础设施及大型城市环保项目提供定制化解决方案。
